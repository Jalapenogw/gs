End of
Dragons

Ten years of the Dragon Cycle come to a head in Cantha — jade, ley-lines, and one very old debt between mother and children.

Released Feb 28, 2022 5 Acts 19 Story Chapters Time Period 1335 AE
Before the story begins

Where things stand

The Icebrood Saga just wrapped: Jormag and Primordus, two of the five Elder Dragons, are dead, and the charr civil war is over. Peace, for once, has actually arrived. But every dragon death dumps raw draconic magic back into Tyria — and Aurene, the young dragon of Crystal and Light bonded to the Commander, has been quietly absorbing it. She's started having visions. All of them point to the same day.

Guild Wars · Prophecies (Core Campaign)

The Searing
and After

A kingdom burns, its exiles find a new home built on a lie, and something far older than either war wakes up underneath it all.

Released Apr 28, 2005 5 Acts 25 Missions Time Period 1070–1072 AE Continent Tyria
Before the story begins

Where things stand

Ascalon is at peace — the kind of peace that exists right before it doesn't. The Charr, a savage nation to the north, have been quiet for years, but King Adelbern knows better than to trust it. You start the game in a tutorial stretch of the world known as "pre-Searing": Ascalon exactly as it looked the day before everything changed, used here to learn the basics and pick a second profession.

This guide covers the core campaign only — no Factions, Nightfall, or Eye of the North. Dip in and out of it; every chapter stands on its own.

Guild Wars · Factions (First Expansion)

Blood of the
Betrayer

A monastery training exercise turns into a plague investigation, and a bodyguard who died two hundred years ago wants back into the world.

Released Apr 28, 2006 5 Acts 12 Missions Time Period 1072 AE Continent Cantha
Before the story begins

Where things stand

Cantha has been closed off from the rest of Tyria for generations, and mostly at peace with itself — aside from the centuries-old rivalry between the forest-dwelling Kurzicks and the sea-faring Luxons, who've disliked each other for so long nobody quite remembers how it started. You begin as a fresh initiate at the Shing Jea Monastery, training under Master Togo, on what's about to become a very bad week for the whole empire.

This tab covers Factions on its own. If you've read the Prophecies or End of Dragons tabs, a couple of names and places here will feel familiar — that's not a coincidence, and it's flagged where it comes up.

Guild Wars · Nightfall (Second Expansion)

The Fall of a
God

A general with a grudge against her own gods, a wasteland no mortal is supposed to survive, and a war that ends with one of the heroes becoming a deity.

Released Oct 27, 2006 5 Acts 18 Missions Time Period 1075 AE Continent Elona
Before the story begins

Where things stand

Elona is a continent split into three very different provinces: peaceful Istan, militarized Kourna, and wealthy Vabbi, loosely held together by an order of protectors called the Sunspears. You begin as a fresh recruit joining that order on Istan, right as Corsair raids on the island start getting suspiciously organized.

This tab covers Nightfall on its own. Like the other tabs, a few names and reveals here connect directly back to Prophecies and Factions — those moments are flagged when they come up.

Guild Wars · Eye of the North (Third Expansion)

Fire Beneath
the Ice

An earthquake cracks open the underside of the world, and three peoples nobody in central Tyria has properly met yet turn out to be exactly who's needed to stop it.

Released Aug 31, 2007 3 Acts 12 Chapters Time Period 1078 AE Continent Tyria
Before the story begins

Where things stand

Six years have passed since the Searing. Ascalon's war with the Charr grinds on unresolved, Kryta is still recovering from the White Mantle's fall, and none of that is what this story is about. Eye of the North is an expansion, not a new campaign — it picks up any existing character (Tyrian, Canthan, or Elonian) and sends them somewhere none of them have been: the frozen far north, where an earthquake has just cracked the world open.

Structurally, this one's a little different from the other three guides here: instead of one straight line of missions, Act II splits into three storylines you can tackle in any order, and it's told more through dungeons and open zones than a strict mission list. This tab reflects that shape rather than forcing a false numbering onto it.

Guild Wars · Beyond (Free Bridge Content)

The Last
Chapters

A kingdom's civil war finally ends, two soldiers find their way back to each other, and a new zealot rises in Cantha promising the same purity Shiro once demanded — with a much more human face.

Released 2010–2012 3 Acts 16 Chapters Time Period 1078–1080 AE Continent Tyria & Cantha
Before the story begins

Where things stand

By the time Beyond starts, the Flameseeker Prophecies have already come true — Prophecies ended with the White Mantle's leader Confessor Dorian dead, and Eye of the North's release of the Titans left their god-like Mursaat patrons nearly extinct. Kryta has fallen into open civil war between the crippled White Mantle and the rebel Shining Blade. Meanwhile, seven years have passed in Cantha since Shiro Tagachi's defeat.

Beyond isn't a purchasable campaign — it's free story content ArenaNet added to close out loose threads before Guild Wars 2. It's the last Guild Wars 1 content chronologically, and it explains a few things GW2 players might wonder about, like why Lion's Arch looks the way it does.

Guild Wars 2 · Core Game

The First
Victory

Two hundred and fifty years after the last Guild Wars story, five very different heroes rise from five very different homelands — and end up fighting the same undead dragon.

Released Aug 28, 2012 8 Chapters 5 Racial Origins Time Period 1325 AE Continent Tyria
Before the story begins

Where things stand

Two and a half centuries have passed since Beyond. The world has moved on: five very different peoples now share Tyria, each with their own capital, their own politics, and their own idea of how to deal with the five Elder Dragons that have reawakened and reshaped the map. You start as an unknown — a soldier, a hunter, a scholar, whatever your background — in whichever of the five home cities matches your character's race.

This is where Guild Wars 2 breaks from the mission-list structure of the earlier tabs: your Personal Story is shaped by your race and the choices you made at character creation, all the way up through level 30. This guide gives each race its own section for the opening chapters, then follows the single shared path everyone converges on afterward — the fight to stop the Elder Dragon Zhaitan.

Guild Wars 2 · Living World Season 1

Scarlet's
War

A brilliant, unhinged sylvari anarchist arms three separate armies against a city that just started feeling hopeful again.

Released 2013 (re-released 2022) 5 Acts 11 Episodes Time Period 1326–1327 AE Continent Tyria
Before the story begins

Where things stand

Zhaitan is dead, the Pact is basking in the first real victory against an Elder Dragon in generations, and Lion's Arch is enjoying a rare stretch of good news. That doesn't last. Living World Season 1 was Guild Wars 2's first ongoing story after launch — originally released in short-lived, unrepeatable chunks between 2013 and 2014, and later rebuilt into five permanent chapters in 2022 so everyone could finally experience it. This guide follows that rebuilt structure.

This is also where several long-running characters get their start: Braham, Rox, Marjory, Kasmeer, and Taimi — the eventual core of what becomes Dragon's Watch — all meet for the first time here, dragged together by one woman's escalating campaign of chaos.

Guild Wars 2 · Living World Season 2

Glint's
Prophecy

A quiet investigation into Scarlet's leftovers turns into a race against a waking Elder Dragon — and uncovers a secret one of the party's closest friends has been keeping since before the Commander ever met her.

Released 2014–2015 5 Acts 8 Episodes Time Period 1327–1328 AE Continent Tyria
Before the story begins

Where things stand

Lion's Arch is still picking up the pieces after Scarlet, and everyone wants to believe her death was the end of it. It wasn't. The roar heard the moment she died is confirmed early here: her rampage disrupted a major ley line and woke the Elder Dragon Mordremoth long before its time — and this whole season is the slow, mounting realization of exactly how bad that is.

This is also where the found family from Season 1 — Braham, Rox, Marjory, Kasmeer, and Taimi — really solidifies, right as Destiny's Edge starts falling apart again in a completely different way.

Guild Wars 2 · First Expansion

Heart of
Thorns

The Pact's fleet is wreckage, its leadership is scattered across a jungle that wants them dead, and the only way out is through — deeper, not back.

Released Oct 23, 2015 4 Acts 16 Chapters Time Period 1328 AE Continent Tyria
Before the story begins

Where things stand

Season 2 ended on the worst possible note: the Pact's entire airship armada, torn apart by Mordremoth's vines above the Heart of Maguuma, its survivors scattered through hostile jungle with no idea whether Trahearne or the rest of Destiny's Edge are even still alive. Heart of Thorns picks up immediately from there — no time skip, no reset, just the Commander and a search party heading in after them.

This is also where the sylvari's darkest secret, uncovered back in Season 2, finally has to be dealt with head-on: they were created by Mordremoth, as an army waiting to be called home.

Guild Wars 2 · Living World Season 3

Glint's
Legacy

A new guild forms to mourn one loss, a god hides in plain sight for over a year, and a two-century-old secret about a martyred cult founder finally comes out.

Released 2016–2017 6 Acts 17 Episodes Time Period 1328–1330 AE Continent Tyria
Before the story begins

Where things stand

Mordremoth is dead, but its release of dragon magic into the world's ley lines didn't just stop there — old, dormant factions and forces are stirring, drawn to the surplus of power. Chief among them: the White Mantle, the Krytan cult that hasn't mattered since the war chronicled in this site's Beyond tab, quietly rebuilding for over two centuries around one goal — reviving their mursaat "god," Lazarus.

This is also where Glint's egg, safeguarded since Heart of Thorns, finally hatches — the direct origin of the character End of Dragons players already know as Aurene.

Guild Wars 2 · Second Expansion

Path of
Fire

How do you stop a god? A rogue Balthazar marches on the Crystal Desert to kill an Elder Dragon and steal its power — which means, for once, the Commander has to protect the very dragon that once destroyed their closest allies.

Released Sep 22, 2017 3 Acts 15 Chapters Time Period 1330 AE Continent Elona
Before the story begins

Where things stand

Balthazar spent all of Living World Season 3 hiding behind a stolen face — and now that the disguise is gone, he's marching openly on the Crystal Desert with one goal: kill the Elder Dragon Kralkatorrik and take its power for himself. That desert isn't empty ground, either. It's Elona, a once-thriving desert nation last visited back in this site's Nightfall tab, now locked under the iron rule of an undead lich-king named Palawa Joko.

It's also personal in a way this story doesn't shy away from: Kralkatorrik is the same Elder Dragon that killed Glint and Snaff and shattered the original Destiny's Edge, years before the Commander ever arrived in Tyria. Saving it anyway is the whole point.

Guild Wars 2 · Living World Season 4

A New
Star

An undead lich out for revenge, a young dragon terrified of a prophecy she can't outrun, and a grandfather made of crystal and grief who just wants the pain to stop.

Released 2017–2019 4 Acts 17 Episodes Time Period 1330–1331 AE Continent Elona & Tyria
Before the story begins

Where things stand

Balthazar is dead, but his death didn't solve anything — it just handed most of his stolen power straight to Kralkatorrik, the same Elder Dragon that killed Glint and shattered the original Destiny's Edge years ago. Aurene absorbed some of that surge too, and she's growing fast. Meanwhile Palawa Joko, abandoned in the afterlife after Dragon's Watch used his own armies against him, is not the type to let that go.

This is also where Braham reappears with a new guild of his own — one he's pointedly also named Destiny's Edge — and where a young asuran entomologist named Gorrik gets pulled into Dragon's Watch's orbit for the first time.

Guild Wars 2 · The Icebrood Saga

Blood &
Ice

A charr celebration turns into a civil war, a young norn's prophecy comes due, and a father has to make a choice no story should ask of anyone.

Released 2019–2020 6 Acts 23 Episodes Time Period 1332–1334 AE Continent Tyria
Before the story begins

Where things stand

Kralkatorrik is dead, Aurene has ascended into a full Elder Dragon, and for once Tyria has a reason to celebrate. Blood Legion Imperator Bangar Ruinbringer throws a party for the occasion — and uses it as cover to make his real move: securing an Elder Dragon of the charr's very own. What follows is the most personal, most consequential season in the whole Living World — a charr civil war that tears one family apart from the inside, and the story that finally explains why Jormag and Primordus never threaten Tyria again by the time of End of Dragons.

This is also the story where Braham Eirsson, introduced all the way back in Season 1, finally steps into the role this whole site has been quietly building toward: the Norn of Prophecy.

Guild Wars 2 · Fourth Expansion

Secrets of the
Obscure

A tower that's watched Tyria in silence for millennia suddenly vanishes — and the reason turns out to be a demon king with a grudge going back centuries longer than the Elder Dragons ever mattered.

Released Aug 22, 2023 6 Acts 19 Chapters Time Period 1336 AE Realm Tyria & Nayos
Before the story begins

Where things stand

Zhaitan, Mordremoth, Kralkatorrik, Jormag, Primordus — five Elder Dragons, all dealt with. Aurene rules as Tyria's new protector, and for the first time in a decade, there's genuinely nothing chasing the Commander. That quiet doesn't last. High above the sleepy hamlet of Garenhoff, an ancient, mysterious tower has watched the entire Dragon Cycle unfold from a safe distance — and it's just vanished without a trace, dragging the Commander into a hidden war that's been running since long before Tyria's dragons ever woke.

This is also where a familiar face gets a proper, emotional send-off: Zojja, of the original Destiny's Edge, finds something here that finally lets her put her past down.

Guild Wars 2 · Fifth Expansion

Janthir
Wilds

A new alliance sends the Wayfinder to befriend a reclusive bear-people in a forgotten corner of the map — and finds a trio of ancient, world-ending giants already living there.

Released Aug 20, 2024 5 Acts 19 Chapters Time Period 1337 AE Realm Janthir & the Mists
Before the story begins

Where things stand

Eparch is dead, the Astral Ward has finally gone public, and Tyria's leaders have formed something new: the Tyrian Alliance, a standing coalition meant to face whatever comes through the weakened Veil next. With Aurene still recovering her strength after Soo-Won's death, that Veil is thinner than it's ever been. The Wayfinder — the Commander's new title after Nayos — is sent to the isolated wilds of Janthir to court a reclusive people called the Lowland Kodan as allies, in a region once ruled by the mursaat and their White Mantle followers.

It doesn't stay a simple diplomatic trip for long. This tab also closes a loop this site has been carrying since the Secrets of the Obscure tab: Mabon, the mursaat wizard who died in Nayos, gets a real, proper farewell here.
